# GrandMart: From Disconnected Operations to One Retail Operating System

A clean, executive-grade corporate web presentation and operational proposal to unify GrandMart's two retail branches (Chennai) and 610 employees using the Zoho ecosystem and ManageEngine Endpoint Central.

## Live Website
- **GitHub Pages:** [https://praveenkumar-2006.github.io/grandmart-zoho-proposal/](https://praveenkumar-2006.github.io/grandmart-zoho-proposal/)

## Executive Highlights
- **Scope:** 2 Branches in Chennai, 610 Staff, ~₹240 Cr Yearly Revenue, 40,000 SKUs, 5,400 daily transactions.
- **Year 1 Total Investment:** ₹96.0 L (Software Subscriptions ₹61.7L + Admin/Support Buffer ₹9.3L + Implementation ₹25.0L).
- **Year 2 Onward Steady-State:** ₹71.0 L / year.
- **Quantified Run-Rate Annual Benefits:** ₹153.0 L / year (₹71.0L Direct Cash + ₹82.0L Staff Productivity).
- **Projected 3-Year ROI:** ~67% (Base Case) with break-even payback in ~13 months.

## Proposal Structure (All 13 Presentation Slides)
1. **Overview & Executive Summary**: Context and core operational baseline.
2. **GrandMart at a Glance**: Revenue model, planning assumptions, and full 9-department headcount allocation.
3. **Six Problems That Compound at Scale**: Inventory blindness, anonymous customers, HR overload, support black holes, reporting lag, and POS downtime risk.
4. **Operating Model & Architecture**: Integrated 3-tier Zoho ecosystem (CRM, Desk, People, Inventory, Books, Analytics) + ManageEngine Endpoint Central.
5. **One Source of Truth Workflows**: Event stream from sales, replenishment, customer complaints, attendance, IT patching, to management reporting.
6. **Product Deep-Dive & Pre-Requisites**: Exact product roles, limits, and pre-go-live readiness checklist.
7. **Zoho vs. Fragmented Tools**: Direct architectural and cost comparison (e.g. Zoho CRM ₹7.2L vs Salesforce ₹39L).
8. **Itemized Financial Budget**: Exact license quotes across all 610 employees and 215 endpoints.
9. **Quantified Value Model**: Interactive filter separating direct cash margin gains from staff time savings.
10. **ROI Sensitivity Analysis**: Interactive modeling across Conservative (-30%), Base Case, and Optimistic (+30%) scenarios.
11. **Risks, Limits, and Data Security**: Concrete mitigation plans for POS API connectors, Books invoice limits, DPDP Act India data center residency, and change management.
12. **90-Day Staged Implementation Plan**: Phase 1 (Foundations), Phase 2 (Retail Core & Pilot), Phase 3 (Multi-Branch Scale) + 4-6 months stabilization.
13. **Management Recommendations**: Recommendation to approve a one-branch pilot with a de-risked ~₹36.0L Phase 1 budget.
